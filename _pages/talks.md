<section id="themes" class="kh-section">
  <div class="kh-section__heading">
    <p class="kh-kicker">Session ideas</p>
    <h2>Six starting points—each can become a keynote, briefing, or workshop.</h2>
  </div>

  <div class="kh-topic-grid kh-topic-grid--sessions">

    <article>
      <span>Business-specific</span>
      <h3>AI Literacy for My Business</h3>
      <p>
        Functional, ethical, and rhetorical AI literacy translated into your
        team’s real workflows, customer interactions, and decision risks.
        A workshop version uses your own cases to identify where capability,
        persuasion, governance, and human judgment matter.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article>
      <span>Early signals</span>
      <h3>Before the Dashboard Moves</h3>
      <p>
        Learn how unstructured text, image, audio, and video can reveal weak
        signals before they become visible in surveys, CRM, sales, or financial
        data. The workshop version turns those signals into a monitoring and
        decision framework for your organisation.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article>
      <span>Interactive / inspirational</span>
      <h3>Are We Really in Control of What We Consume?</h3>
      <p>
        An interactive exploration of recommendation systems, attention, time,
        data, preferences, and autonomy. Audiences examine both the benefits and
        hidden trade-offs of AI-mediated consumption without being told what
        conclusion to reach.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article>
      <span>Responsible AI</span>
      <h3>Auditable AI at Scale</h3>
      <p>
        How can teams use LLMs at speed without treating model output as truth?
        We unpack gold standards, human validation, model-auditor layers,
        reliability checks, and governance through practical examples.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article>
      <span>Strategy</span>
      <h3>AI Without the Hype: What Changes the Decision?</h3>
      <p>
        Separate technical capability, adoption, business value, and human
        impact so AI initiatives can be judged by the decisions they improve.
        Teams leave with a simple framework for deciding where AI creates
        advantage—and where it merely creates activity.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article>
      <span>Leadership / inspirational</span>
      <h3>What Should We Automate, Augment, or Keep Human?</h3>
      <p>
        A forward-looking session on the future allocation of judgment between
        people and AI. It helps leaders make autonomy, accountability, creativity,
        and trust explicit design choices rather than accidental consequences.
      </p>
    </article>

  </div>
</section>
