You can read this website without allowing analytics.

With your permission, I use Google Analytics to measure page visits, downloads
and clicks on external links. This helps me understand which research and resources
people find useful. Analytics remains off until you choose **Allow analytics**.
The site remembers your choice in your browser for up to 180 days.

The site's tracking code excludes email addresses and URL query strings from
the events it sends. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.
Google processes analytics data under its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

<button type="button" class="kh-analytics-settings" data-kh-analytics-settings>Change analytics choices</button>

Choosing **No thanks** stops analytics. If analytics was already running,
the page reloads to apply your choice and removes its analytics cookies.

The [AI Empowerment Observatory](https://observatory.hamelberg-ai.com/privacy/)
and [the Brief](https://kedmahamelberg-ai.github.io/aieo-brief/privacy/)
have their own privacy information and choices.

If you email me, I use the information you send to respond to your enquiry.
For privacy questions, contact [kedma@hamelberg-ai.com](mailto:kedma@hamelberg-ai.com).
