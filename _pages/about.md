<section class="kh-about-principles">
  <p class="kh-kicker">What connects my work</p>
  <h2>Three principles I keep coming back to.</h2>

  <div class="kh-principle-grid">
    <article>
      <span>01</span>
      <h3>Unstructured data can be an early signal</h3>
      <p>
        People speak, search, post, watch, react, and create before those changes
        appear in a dashboard. I use multimodal digital behavior to make those
        shifts visible earlier.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article>
      <span>02</span>
      <h3>AI output is not ground truth</h3>
      <p>
        Speed matters, but so do validation and accountability. My workflows use
        theory, human gold standards, model comparison, and audit layers rather
        than treating automated classification as truth.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article>
      <span>03</span>
      <h3>Complexity should become usable</h3>
      <p>
        A method matters when people can understand what it changes for a
        decision. Research, public tools, executive briefings, and teaching are
        different ways of making rigorous evidence usable.
      </p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
